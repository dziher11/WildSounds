2. Information Collection and Use

Green Mouse does not collect any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII) through Wild Animals Sounds.

The game functions completely offline on your Android device:

  • No User Account: You are not required to register, create an account, or log in.
  • Local Gameplay Preferences Only: The background music on/off setting is stored strictly locally on your device via Android's private SharedPreferences storage.
  • No Server Communication: No gameplay progress, settings, or device details are ever transmitted over the internet to any external server or third party.

3. 100% Offline Application

Wild Animals Sounds operates entirely offline. All game assets, wild animal illustrations, animal sounds, sound effects, and background music are packaged directly inside the application installation file. The game does not connect to the internet, does not use external web services, and does not require an active network connection.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Wild Animals Sounds contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect, track, or share user data:

  • Zero Advertisements: Wild Animals Sounds is completely ad-free. All third-party advertising SDKs (such as AdMob) have been completely removed.
  • Zero Analytics or Trackers: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, or any other tracking libraries.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Because no data is collected, no data is ever shared with, sold to, or accessible by advertisers or third parties.

5. Device Permissions

Wild Animals Sounds requires zero (0) device permissions:

  • No INTERNET permission.
  • No ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE or ACCESS_WIFI_STATE permission.
  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • No Location access (neither precise nor coarse).
  • No Contacts, Phone, or SMS access.
  • No External Storage reading or writing.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Wild Animals Sounds is designed for children and families:

  • The game complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and Google Play's Families Policy.
  • We do not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable).
  • Parents and guardians can have full confidence that children's privacy is respected and protected while playing.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All locally stored game preferences (such as the background music toggle) reside strictly in your device's private sandbox storage:

  • You can clear all local game data at any time via your device settings: Settings > Apps > Wild Animals Sounds > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the game automatically and permanently deletes all locally stored data.

8. Security

Because Wild Animals Sounds does not collect or transmit information over the internet, your gameplay data cannot be intercepted, leaked, or accessed remotely. Data safety is guaranteed by the Android operating system's application sandbox security model.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
